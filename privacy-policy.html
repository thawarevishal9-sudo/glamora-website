Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, Glamora stores the information needed to operate the account, such as your name, email address, account role and optional Instagram handle. Passwords are stored as cryptographic password hashes and are not stored as plain text.

2. Bookings and payments

Booking details such as artist, service, date, time, amount and booking status are stored so the platform can manage appointments. If live Razorpay payments are enabled, payment processing is handled by Razorpay. Glamora stores payment identifiers needed to reconcile a booking, not your payment card credentials.

3. Portfolio media

Artists may upload portfolio images. Uploaded files are stored by the Glamora application and associated with the artist profile.

4. Contact messages

Information submitted through the contact form is stored so the Glamora team can respond to the request.

5. Sessions

After login, Glamora uses a secure HTTP-only session cookie so you can return to the website without entering your password on every visit. Sessions expire after the configured session period.

6. Visitor counter

Glamora maintains a simple site visit counter. The counter starts at 100 for this project and increments when the website records a visit. It is not presented as a count of unique people.

7. Data retention and deletion

Account, booking, review and message data remain in the application's database while needed to operate the platform. For a production launch, the final retention and deletion process should be configured for the operating jurisdiction and hosting environment.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the platform's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Glamora contact form or the contact details published with the final custom-domain deployment.
